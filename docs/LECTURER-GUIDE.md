# A guide for lecturers

This is a short guide to your profile on the Department of Mathematics website. No technical
knowledge is needed.

## Accepting your invite

You will receive an email with a link inviting you to join the site. Click it and choose a
password (at least 10 characters). Once that is done, sign in at the site with your email and new
password.

## Finding your profile

After signing in, you will see "My profile" in the menu. This is the only place you need to work
in. Everything you add here can later appear on your public page once it is published.

## Filling in your profile

- **Photo**: click "Choose or upload", pick a photo of yourself, and add a short description of
  it (this is required, for accessibility and search engines).
- **Honorific**: for example "Dr." or "Prof."
- **Bio**: a short paragraph about yourself and your work. You can use bold, italic, lists, links
  and mathematical notation.
- **Research areas**: tick every research area that applies to you.
- **Office location and office hours**: optional, shown on your public page if filled in.
- **Public email and phone**: optional. These are the only contact details ever shown publicly.
  The email address you sign in with is never shown to the public, no matter what.
- **Google Scholar, ORCID, ResearchGate, LinkedIn**: optional links. Each must be a proper link to
  that exact service (for example, your ORCID iD must be a valid ORCID number).
- **Students and supervision**: a section for prospective students, if you want one.
- **Publications**: add each one with its title, authors, venue, year, and optionally a link and
  a DOI. Use the up and down buttons to reorder them.
- **CV**: upload a PDF, up to 5 MB.

Every time you click Save, your current answers in every field are stored (not just the one you
just changed), so the form always shows the complete current state. A "Version history" button
lets you see earlier saved versions and restore one if you need to undo a mistake.

## Requesting publishing

Once you have added a photo, a bio and at least one research area, a "Request publishing" button
appears. Click it when you are ready to go public. An administrator will review and publish your
profile; you will not be able to tell exactly when, but your page will then appear at
`/lecturer/your-name`.

After your profile is first published, any further changes you save go live immediately, without
needing another approval. An administrator can still unpublish your page or restore an earlier
version if needed.

## Changing a photo or your CV later

Go back to "My profile" at any time, choose a new photo or CV file, and save. The old file is
replaced.

## Changing your password

Go to "Change password" in the menu. You will need your current password. Choosing a new one
signs any other device you are signed in on out, within a few minutes.

## If something goes wrong

If you cannot sign in, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page, or contact the department
administrator to resend your invite link.
