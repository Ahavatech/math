# Journal author guide

This guide explains how to submit a manuscript to the OAU Mathematics journal.

## Before you submit

- Read the author guidelines at `/journal/guidelines` on the journal website.
- Ensure your manuscript is anonymised: remove your name, affiliation and acknowledgements from the main manuscript file. These go only in the title page file.
- Accepted file types: PDF (.pdf) and Word (.docx). Maximum 25 MB per file.

## Step 1: Register or log in

Go to the journal home page and select Register (new users) or Log in (returning users). Your account is separate from any staff or alumni account on the main department website.

## Step 2: Start a submission

From your dashboard, select New submission. You can save a draft at any step and return to it later. Drafts not updated for 60 days are automatically removed.

## Step 3: Fill in the submission form

The form has five steps:

1. **Article details** - article type, title, abstract, and keywords.
2. **Authors** - name, affiliation and email for each author. Select who the corresponding author is.
3. **Files** - upload your anonymised manuscript file. Optionally upload a title page, supplementary materials, and a cover letter. Confirm that the manuscript is properly anonymised.
4. **Author agreement** - read and accept the journal author agreement.
5. **Review** - check everything before submitting.

You cannot submit without a manuscript file.

## After submission

Once submitted, your manuscript moves to desk screening by the editor. You will receive an email when your submission is received, and further emails when the status changes.

You can track the status of all your submissions from your dashboard.

### Status meanings

- **Draft** - not yet submitted; only you can see it.
- **Received** - submitted and awaiting desk screening.
- **With the editor** - the editor is reviewing the submission.
- **Needs your attention** - the editor has returned the manuscript and requires corrections before it can proceed. You will receive a message explaining what to address.
- **In peer review** - the manuscript has passed desk screening and is with external reviewers.
- **Not accepted** - the manuscript was not accepted. An editor message is available on your dashboard.
- **Withdrawn** - you withdrew the submission.

## Resubmitting after return

If your submission is returned for corrections, you can upload revised files from the submission detail page. Once you have addressed all the points, resubmit.

## Withdrawing a submission

Contact the journal office to withdraw a submission.

## Questions

For questions about the submission process, contact the editorial office using the details on the journal About page.
